Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

At ORM Calc (onerepmaxcalculator.me), your privacy is not an afterthought; it is the core architecture of our platform. We believe you should be able to calculate your strength metrics without sacrificing your personal data.

1. Our Data Philosophy

Most modern web applications rely on a server database to function. ORM Calc is different. It is a 100% static, client-side web application. This means the code runs entirely in your web browser. We do not have a backend database storing user profiles, passwords, or training logs.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

To use all 35 of our tools, you do not need to provide:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number.
  • Your physical address or billing information (the site is 100% free).
  • Any health data, biometric data, or training history.

3. How LocalStorage Works

If you use the "Tracker" feature to log your workouts, or if you adjust plate inventories, this data is saved using your browser's localStorage API.

  • Device Only: This data is stored locally on your hard drive or mobile device.
  • No Transmission: It is never sent over the internet to our servers.
  • You Are in Control: If you clear your browser's cache or site data, your logs will be deleted. We highly recommend using the Export CSV button in the tracker to save backups.

4. Analytics and Tracking

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any other invasive third-party tracking scripts. We do not track your behavior across other websites. We do use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-first, cookieless analytics service. It does not collect personal data, does not fingerprint visitors, and cannot identify individuals — it only produces aggregate counts of page views and visits. Cloudflare processes anonymized request data for this purpose under its own privacy policy. No analytics cookies are set, and no cross-site profiles are built.

5. Third-Party Hosting & Server Logs

Our website is hosted on Cloudflare's global edge network. When you visit onerepmaxcalculator.me, Cloudflare processes your request and may log standard technical data:

  • IP Address (used for DDoS protection and routing).
  • Browser type and operating system.
  • Referring URL.
  • Timestamp of access.
This data is retained by Cloudflare for security and operational purposes, not for marketing or profiling. You can read Cloudflare's Privacy Policy here.

6. GDPR and CCPA Compliance

Because we do not collect, store, or sell personal data, ORM Calc inherently complies with the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). We have no personal data to sell, share, or delete upon request. To exercise control over your data, simply clear your browser's local storage.

7. Children's Online Privacy Protection

Our site is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly solicit or collect information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements or website features. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised.

9. Contact Information

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us via our Contact Page or email us directly at privacy@onerepmaxcalculator.me.
